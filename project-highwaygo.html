ABOUT
THE PROJECT

HighwayGo LK is a full-stack mobile application built for the Sri Lankan highway bus travel market. The core purpose is to digitize the entire bus booking experience — from searching available buses on a specific route, to selecting seats, processing payment, and tracking the bus live on a map.

The app serves two distinct user types: passengers who want to book intercity bus tickets, and bus owners who want to manage their fleet, routes, and bookings through a dedicated owner portal. Both journeys run within a single cross-platform codebase with completely independent authentication systems.

Sri Lanka's intercity bus industry historically had very little digital infrastructure. Passengers had to physically visit stations or rely on phone calls to find buses on specific routes. HighwayGo LK solves both sides — a seamless mobile booking experience for passengers and a complete management dashboard for operators.

The project was built incrementally across 25 development versions — from a bare Expo skeleton in v1 through authentication, search, seat selection, payment, owner flow, live GPS tracking, and finally production-level polish including animated splash screens, skeleton loaders, and FlatList performance optimizations.

Dual User Flows
01 · Passenger Flow
PASSENGER JOURNEY
  • JWT auth with AsyncStorage persistent sessions
  • Live route search with API-populated city modals
  • Native date picker with past-date prevention
  • Interactive seat grid with real availability data
  • Animated credit card payment UI with live preview
  • Digital booking ticket with QR placeholder
  • PDF receipt download from booking success screen
  • Live bus tracking on Google Maps — auto-refresh every 10s
  • Full booking history with per-trip details
02 · Owner Flow
OWNER DASHBOARD
  • Separate owner auth — independent token from passenger
  • Fleet management — add, edit, delete buses
  • Bus image upload via expo-image-picker (base64)
  • Optional return route configuration toggle
  • Live GPS location sharing from driver's device
  • Auto-update location broadcast every 10 seconds
  • Booking analytics with total revenue stats
  • Full passenger booking details per bus
  • Owner profile with business registration details
