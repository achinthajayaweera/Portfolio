ABOUT
THE DESIGN

Agri Manager Pro is a full mobile UI/UX design for a digital agricultural management platform aimed at Sri Lankan farmers. The app brings together several critical services that farmers typically need to access separately — microloans, agricultural inputs, machinery, and financial tracking — into a single, easy-to-navigate mobile experience.

The platform's core financial feature is its microloan system, which allows farmers to apply for short-term agricultural loans directly through the app. Farmers can view available loan schemes, submit applications, track approval status, and manage repayments — removing the need for physical bank visits that are often inaccessible in rural areas.

Beyond finance, the app covers fertilizer and input services — allowing farmers to browse available agricultural products, place orders, and track deliveries. A dedicated machinery hire module lets farmers locate and book farm equipment by category, with machine and labor profiles showing availability and pricing.

The design also includes crop tracking, location services, expense management, and agri insurance screens — giving farmers a complete digital toolkit for managing their operations. The visual language uses a strong green-dominant palette that communicates growth, nature, and trust, with clean card-based layouts optimised for low-literacy and rural users.
