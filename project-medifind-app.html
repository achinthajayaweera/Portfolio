ABOUT
THE PROJECT

MediFind is a smart healthcare platform developed as a Software Development Group Project (SDGP) at the Informatics Institute of Technology. The platform addresses a real-world problem in Sri Lanka — the difficulty patients face when searching for prescribed medicines across multiple pharmacies, comparing availability, and arranging delivery.

The system allows patients to upload a prescription using their mobile device or manually enter medication details. Advanced AI-powered prescription processing extracts medicine information automatically and queries registered pharmacy databases using location-based services to find nearby pharmacies with the required stock.

Rather than requiring users to contact multiple pharmacies manually, MediFind's intelligent pharmacy matching engine automatically compares nearby options based on availability, pricing, distance, and delivery charges — presenting the best match and allowing direct in-app ordering.

The platform includes prescription authenticity verification through doctor-issued codes, medical seal recognition, and duplicate prescription detection. Automated refill reminders notify patients when medication is running low — critical for patients managing chronic conditions.
