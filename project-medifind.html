About the Project

WHAT IS
THIS?

Medifind is a healthcare discovery platform built as part of the Software Development Group Project (SDGP) module during the 2nd year of my BSc Software Engineering degree at IIT Colombo / University of Westminster.

The platform is designed to help users in Sri Lanka find and connect with nearby doctors, clinics, and pharmacies — solving a real-world problem where patients struggle to identify the right medical resources quickly in an emergency or for routine care.

I was responsible for the entire front-end of the web platform — building the landing page, all UI sections, and the multi-device responsive showcase. The design features a gradient animated hero title, smooth scroll transitions, phone and MacBook mockup sections, and interactive feature highlights.

The landing page includes 9 different device mockups (iPhone 17 Pro Max models in various angles) plus a MacBook model section — giving clients and reviewers a real feel of how the app looks across devices. Every section is carefully animated and purposefully structured.
