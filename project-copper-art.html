About the Project

WHAT IS
THIS?

Copper Art Antiques is a premium antique products shop located in Atulcota, Sri Lanka. The owner needed a digital presence that matched the elegance and heritage of their physical store — not just a basic website, but an experience.

I built a fully animated landing page that opens with a custom-made 3D video loader, transitions into a cinematic hero section, and guides visitors through the product collection with scroll-triggered animations throughout.

The site features a 3D product model (animated bronze vessel) embedded using a GLTF/3D module, giving the products a physical, tangible feel on screen. Every animation is timed to feel premium — not fast and flashy, but slow and confident.

The project was built entirely from scratch using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no page builders. Every line is hand-coded for performance and precision.
